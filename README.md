# Heber City, Utah to Houston, Texas — the gentle route

A five-day route for a 68–72 ft truck-and-fifth-wheel combination, chosen to
minimise mountain driving rather than miles.

Every road is listed in order, and every stretch of 2% grade or steeper is
measured — elevation sampled every 1.5 miles along the real routed geometry
(SRTM 90 m via opentopodata.org), not estimated from town elevations.

Compared against the obvious alternative, US-50 across Colorado over Monarch
Pass. Same corridor, 3,700 ft more climbing.

Published at: https://texaspja.github.io/gentle-route-utah-texas/
