Five days home, and not one pass above 7,500 feet.

The road

Solid line is the route. Dashed is Highway 50 through Colorado. The Continental Divide crossing on this route is on I-40 west of Albuquerque, and you will not notice it.

Why not Highway 50

The same journey, drawn by altitude instead of geography. This is the whole argument in one picture.

Day by day

Two ways to drive it. Same roads, same measured grades — the difference is where you sleep and whether you get home Friday or Saturday.

The quicker one. Monday does the whole mountain section in a single 6.2-hour push and you are home Friday evening. Every night has a length-verified park.

The easier one. Monday drops from 6.2 hours to 4.1 — the same Provo Canyon, Soldier Summit and Price Canyon, but parked by mid-afternoon. Nothing all week tops 5.7 hours, and it is 67 miles shorter, because the last night on US-287 skips the Weatherford detour. Four of the five nights are now length-verified; only Alvord still needs a call.

Worth knowing

Stay off US-550 and out of Durango

An earlier draft of this route ran through Durango and down US-550. It does not any more — Tuesday turns south at Cortez and stays on US-491 to Gallup. That avoids the Million Dollar Highway above Durango entirely, and it avoids the 164-mile stretch of US-550 where the only town, Cuba, has a park that tops out at 63 feet.

Thursday: do not take the shortcut

Routing apps will try to cut the corner from US-287 to Weatherford on FM 52, FM 1885 and FM 920 — 23 miles of two-lane farm road. It saves 40 minutes and is not worth it at 72 feet. Stay on US-287 into Fort Worth, then I-20 west.

Moab on Labor Day is the one real risk

Three things land on the same weekend: the holiday, the Moab Music Festival (2–18 September) and the Red Rock 4-Wheelers Labor Day Safari. If the six-day plan appeals, book Green River instead — Shady Acres publishes 100 ft pull-throughs on its own site, the best-confirmed length anywhere on either plan, and it is only 50 miles short of Moab.

Nothing is booked

None of these five parks could be checked for live availability, and the 7th is Labor Day. Call all of them, and quote your hitched length, not the trailer length.

Wind on US-287

Thursday’s Amarillo to Fort Worth run has a documented rollover risk for high-sided vehicles. Every route home shares this stretch, so it is not a reason to choose differently — just check the forecast.

Monsoon runs to 30 September

Flash flooding in the washes along US-491 and US-550. Colorado’s fire season is also flagged elevated for 2026 — check CDOT the week you leave.

Thin coverage

Expect real cell gaps between Monticello and Cuba. Download the navigation for that leg before you set off Tuesday morning.
